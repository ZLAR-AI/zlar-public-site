The boundary

ZLAR governs defined routed actions.

ZLAR does not govern thought, model output, or every path through a machine. Governance exists only where a named action is routed through the defined boundary.

Monitoring after an action is not authority. A score is not permission. Human accountability becomes structural when the action can stop before effect and the decision can be verified afterward.
