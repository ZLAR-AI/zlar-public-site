Privacy boundary

Learn from the site without profiling its visitors.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is active. It gives ZLAR aggregate traffic and site-performance measurements without identifying or profiling individual visitors.

What is measured

Effective September 27, 2026, ZLAR uses Cloudflare Web Analytics on zlar.ai. The browser loads Cloudflare's analytics beacon and sends measurements to Cloudflare's Web Analytics endpoint.

The measurement boundary is limited to aggregate site improvement:

  • Page views and aggregate visit counts.
  • Page path and referring website.
  • Country, browser, operating-system, and device categories.
  • Page-load timing and Core Web Vitals.

How privacy is protected

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors' personal data. It does not use cookies or local storage for analytics, does not fingerprint individuals, does not track people across sites, and does not record URL query strings. ZLAR receives aggregate reports, not a list of identifiable visitors.

Cloudflare retains unsampled beacon data for seven days, then stores a sampled aggregate. The dashboard makes up to six months of analytics available. ZLAR does not export raw visitor events or build person-level profiles from this data.

Permanent refusals

ZLAR will not use website analytics to collect or derive:

  • Clipboard contents, form keystrokes, session replay, or screen recordings.
  • Stored raw IP addresses, device fingerprints, or persistent visitor identifiers.
  • Precise location, cross-site behavior, or identity-deanonymization.
  • Personal, customer, patient, employee, government, credential, or source information.

AI-use boundary

AI analysis may use aggregated counts and trends only. Raw visitor events, provider credentials, free text, and person-level profiles are outside the permitted AI input boundary. Any expansion requires a new public notice and a separate authorization decision.

Contact information

Information voluntarily sent by email is used to evaluate and respond to the inquiry. The public-safe contact boundary explains what not to send.
