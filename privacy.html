Privacy

Privacy.

This site counts visits in aggregate. It never profiles you.

What we measure

Cloudflare Web Analytics is active. Effective September 27, 2026, zlar.ai uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. Your browser loads Cloudflare’s small analytics script, which sends measurements to Cloudflare. We see only:

  • page views and how many visits there were;
  • which page was visited and which website you came from;
  • country, browser, operating system and device type;
  • how quickly pages load.

What it doesn’t do

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data. It does not use cookies or local storage for analytics, does not fingerprint people, does not track anyone across sites, and does not record the query part of web addresses. We receive summaries, not a list of visitors.

How long it’s kept

Cloudflare keeps the unsampled measurements for seven days, then a sampled summary. Its dashboard shows up to six months. We don’t export raw visitor events or build profiles of individual people.

What we will never collect

  • what’s on your clipboard, your keystrokes, session replays or screen recordings;
  • stored raw IP addresses, device fingerprints or lasting visitor identifiers;
  • your precise location, what you do on other sites, or anything meant to identify you;
  • personal, customer, patient, employee, government, password or source-code information.

AI and this data

Any AI analysis of this site’s traffic uses aggregated counts and trends only. Raw visitor events, account credentials, free text and individual profiles are never given to AI. Changing that would need a new public notice first.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use what you send to read and answer your message.
