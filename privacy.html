Privacy boundary

Learn from the site without profiling its visitors.

Analytics is not active. No analytics beacon or visitor event collector is included in this public artifact.

Planned measurement

ZLAR intends to use Plausible Analytics only after configuration, retention, processor terms, and an opt-out path are verified. Before activation, this page will state the effective date and final settings.

The permitted measurement boundary is limited to aggregated site improvement:

  • Page views and coarse visit counts.
  • Referrer and non-personal campaign codes.
  • Coarse country, browser, and device categories.
  • Engagement-time buckets and scroll thresholds.
  • Contact, outbound-link, and download-link clicks.
  • Copy or share events identified only by public page section, never by copied text or destination.

Permanent refusals

ZLAR will not use website analytics to collect or derive:

  • Clipboard contents, form keystrokes, session replay, or screen recordings.
  • Stored raw IP addresses, device fingerprints, or persistent visitor identifiers.
  • Precise location, cross-site behavior, or identity-deanonymization.
  • Personal, customer, patient, employee, government, credential, or source information.

AI-use boundary

Future AI analysis may use aggregated counts and trends only. Raw visitor events, provider credentials, free text, and person-level profiles are outside the permitted AI input boundary. Any expansion requires a new public notice and a separate authorization decision.

Contact information

Information voluntarily sent by email is used to evaluate and respond to the inquiry. The public-safe contact boundary explains what not to send.
